BEHIND THE SCORE

Transparent by design.

The score combines the absolute increase with the log of the smoothed rate ratio. Volume and growth thresholds suppress small samples.

Terms group related activity deterministically. Shared terms do not prove that posts describe the same event.

Activity spikes are observations, not claims of truth, importance or virality. Deleted posts are excluded from evidence.Refreshes every 15 seconds
